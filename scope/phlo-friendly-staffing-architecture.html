01 · Where Friendly Staffing is today

The work runs on paper, two separate databases and WhatsApp. Phlo brings it into one system.

Today
  • Paper registration forms, keyed into each office's own spreadsheet
  • Orders arrive by phone, text, email and supervisor WhatsApp groups
  • Staff search their own office's records and call workers one by one
  • Site rules passed on verbally, so workers say they were never told
  • Weekly timesheets handwritten, signed and faxed on Monday morning
  • Hours re-entered by hand before payroll runs in QuickBooks
With Phlo
  • One validated worker database shared by both offices
  • Every order recorded once, whatever channel it arrives on
  • A ranked shortlist in seconds, with bulk offers and tracked replies
  • Site instructions sent automatically on every confirmation
  • Employer timesheets checked against assignments and pay rules
  • Verified hours sent to QuickBooks, deductions shown back

02 · The two phases at a glance

Phase 1 runs operations end to end. Phase 2 takes over timekeeping, payroll and invoicing.

Phase 1 · Operations
  • Worker registration and validation · employee code at Ready to Work
  • Employer order intake · online form plus staff entry
  • AI-assisted shortlisting · ranked by eligibility and fit
  • Offers, replies, employer approval, dispatch
  • Replacements, no-shows and same-day changes
  • Employer timesheets verified, pay and bill rates applied
  • Weekly file to QuickBooks, deductions shown back
  • Data migration from both offices
Phase 2 · Time, pay and billing
  • Friendly Staffing's own timekeeping · tablet at site
  • Digital approval of hours by the employer
  • Payroll in Phlo · replaces QuickBooks
  • CPP, EI, tax, vacation and holiday pay, pay stubs
  • CRA remittances, T4, ROE, WSIB
  • Invoicing with HST and receivables follow-up
  • Worker portal to view and apply for openings
  • Full margin and profitability reporting
In Phase 1, QuickBooks keeps calculating payroll and statutory deductions. Phlo sends it verified hours and shows the results.

06 · Module inventory: Phase 1

Thirteen modules on one shared foundation.

M1
Worker registration and validation
Online form, document and permit checks, consent, Ready to Work, employee code.
M2
Central worker database
Both offices. Skills, availability, past pay, documents, assignment history.
M3
Client, site and position master
Bill rate, default pay rate, requirements, break rules, site instructions.
M4
Employer portal and order intake
Online order form, staff entry for other channels, same-day changes.
M5
AI-assisted shortlisting
Ranked by eligibility and fit. Never ranks on age, gender or health.
M6
Communication hub
Bulk offers, replies, call prompts, reminders, full message log with workers and employers.
M7
Employer approval
Shortlisted profiles shared, approvals recorded per worker.
M8
Dispatch and replacements
Confirmations, assignment details, daily list, no-shows, walk-offs, replacements.
M9
Timesheet capture and verification
Employer sheet captured and checked against assignments and pay rules.
M10
Rates and earnings
Pay and bill rate × hours, regular and overtime, minimum-wage check.
M11
QuickBooks connection
Weekly hours file out, deductions and net pay back in.
M12
Operations and margin reports
Fill rate, open orders, time to fill, no-shows, margin before employer costs.
M13
Data migration
Both offices' records imported once at go-live.
Base
Foundation
User roles, audit trail, encrypted documents, record retention, accessible employer pages.

06 · Module inventory: Phase 2

Six modules that complete the platform and retire QuickBooks payroll.

N1
Own timekeeping
A tablet at the client site with the day's list. Records punches and breaks, confirms each one to the worker and shows live arrival counts.
N2
Digital hours approval
Our record compared with the employer's. The employer approves online instead of signing and faxing.
N3
Payroll
TD1 forms, CPP, EI, income tax, vacation and holiday pay, pay stubs, deposit file, CRA remittances, T4, ROE and WSIB.
N4
Invoicing and receivables
Invoices from approved billable hours plus HST, conversion-fee invoices, receivables tracking and reminders.
N5
Worker portal
Workers log in to view openings, apply, and see their shifts, hours and pay stubs.
N6
Profitability reporting
Margin after employer costs, and invoiced against collected revenue for each client.

08 · What we need from you

A few inputs from your side.

01
A sample of your weekly payroll Excel
The file you prepare for QuickBooks today. It sets the exact format Phlo sends and receives.
02
Preferred messaging channel
SMS, WhatsApp Business or email for job offers and reminders.
03
Temp-to-perm conversion terms
The fee and the period it applies to, so Phlo tracks each worker's first day at each client.
04
The current registration form and pay practices
The latest form version, how often workers are paid, and how breaks and short shifts are paid at each client.
