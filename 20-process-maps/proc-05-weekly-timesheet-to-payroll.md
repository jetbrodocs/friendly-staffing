# Weekly Timesheet to Payroll and Invoicing

## Process Overview

- **Purpose:** Turn each week's worked hours into accurate pay for workers and accurate invoices for clients.
- **Trigger:** The work week ends (Saturday; weeks run Sunday to Saturday).
- **End condition:**
  - **Phase 1:** workers are paid through the bank upload file generated by Phlo, each client is invoiced from Phlo, and the amounts are recorded in QuickBooks accounts.
  - **Build later:** receivables are tracked in Phlo.
- **Frequency:** Weekly, for every worker at every client. Payroll and invoicing both run weekly. Vacation pay runs once a year.
- **Typical duration:** Timesheets arrive from Monday morning. Pay day is the following Friday, six days after the week ends.

## Current Process (as-is)

1. A timekeeper at each client site manages punch-ins and punch-outs every day. Workers initial each day; the supervisor signs each page. At some clients, Friendly Staffing types the sheet and the client signs it off.
2. At the end of the week, the timesheet arrives at Friendly Staffing as a PDF or fax. Example: 17 pages, about 115 workers, Molinaro's, week of 2026-08-10.
3. Staff read the sheets and enter each worker's hours into the **payroll Excel** in four columns: method A regular and overtime, method B regular and overtime. Overtime is above 44 hours.
4. The Excel looks up each worker's mode and rates and calculates **gross pay** per method: regular hours × rate, plus overtime hours × 1.5 × rate. Rate B is 90% of rate A.
5. Staff key each worker's pay into **QuickBooks**, which calculates the method A deductions (CPP, EI, income tax) and produces the pay stub. Method B pay is hours × rate with no deductions.
6. Staff key each payment again into the **bank portal**, using the net figures from QuickBooks. About 350 payments a week. There is no upload file.
7. Staff use the same payroll Excel to prepare **client invoices** in QuickBooks: one line per position, with a bill rate made by adding a margin to the pay rate, plus extra charges such as safety shoes or consulting fees, plus 13% HST.
8. Once a year, staff pay each worker's accrued **vacation pay** (October to September) as a separate QuickBooks pay run.
9. When a worker leaves, staff issue an **ROE** on ROE Web. At year end, **T4 slips** are produced. [UNKNOWN: whether T4s and remittances come from QuickBooks today]

## Phase 1 Process (to-be)

Decisions 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04: everything related to payroll comes from Phlo. QuickBooks stays for bookkeeping and accounting only. Timekeeping and the bank upload file are separate modules.

1. Hours are captured for the week:
   - **Phlo timekeeping module:** the timekeeper marks attendance on her phone: present or absent, punches in and out, and breaks. Workers sign for their hours on their own phones. No tablets. [UNKNOWN: the detailed attendance workflow is still to be designed]
   - **Sites not yet on the module:** staff enter the signed timesheet in Phlo against the assignments, or Phlo produces the typed sheet for the client to sign, as Friendly Staffing does today.
2. Phlo checks the hours:
   - worked hours match a confirmed assignment
   - break rules for the client and position, at or above the ESA eating-period minimum
   - overtime after 44 hours, counted across **all clients** the worker worked for that week
   - minimum pay rules, e.g. the three-hour rule when a shift is cut short
   - study-permit weekly hour cap
   - pay rate at or above Ontario minimum wage ($17.95/hr from 2026-10-01)
   - corrections carried from the previous week
3. Phlo flags any exceptions.
   - **If exceptions:** go to Exception A.
   - **If none:** go to step 4.
4. The employer confirms the week's hours. [UNKNOWN: in Phlo, or by signing the summary as today]
5. Phlo splits each worker's hours by payment mode, using the worker's set split. The split stays the same week to week; staff can change it when the worker asks.
6. Phlo calculates gross pay per method from the **pay rate**: regular hours × rate, plus overtime hours × overtime rate. Other earnings types: statutory holiday pay and bonus [UNKNOWN: how each is decided today].
7. Phlo calculates net pay:
   - **Method A:** CPP, EI and income tax using the CRA payroll deduction formulas (federal and Ontario) and the worker's TD1 and TD1ON. Phlo adds 4% to the worker's vacation pay balance.
   - **Method B:** hours × rate, no deductions.
8. Staff review the payroll run and finalise it.
9. Phlo produces each worker's **pay stub**: hours, rates, gross, deductions, net and year-to-date by payment method.
10. Phlo produces the **bank upload file**. Staff upload it to the bank portal, which pays every worker. No workers are paid outside the bank.
11. Phlo pushes the week's final amounts to **QuickBooks accounts**, which records the transactions.
12. Phlo produces each client's weekly **invoice** (decided 2026-11-04):
    - billable hours × **bill rate** (pay rate plus the margin set for the client and position), regular and overtime, one line per position
    - extra charges, such as safety shoes and consulting fees
    - conversion fees, inside the 6-month window
    - 13% HST and the GST/HST number

    Staff review and send it. Phlo pushes it to QuickBooks accounts.
13. Phlo sends the hours summary to the employer, and the pay stub to each worker.
14. Phlo updates the margin report: (bill rate − pay rate) × hours by worker, order, client and week, before employer costs.

### Exception A: Hours Do Not Check

A1. Staff review the flagged line: missing punch, hours without an assignment, a break below the minimum, or a study-permit cap breach.
A2. Staff resolve it with the employer. The conversation is logged.
A3. Staff correct the entry. Return to step 2.

### Annual: Vacation Pay Run

V1. At the end of the vacation year (30 September), Phlo totals each method A worker's accrued vacation pay.
V2. Staff review and finalise a vacation pay run. Phlo calculates the deductions and produces stubs and the bank file as in steps 7 to 11.
V3. When a worker leaves, Phlo pays out the accrued balance and records it on the ROE.

### Statutory Files (Phase 1, decided 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04)

Phlo produces these. Friendly Staffing uploads the files and makes the payments outside Phlo.
- CRA remittance amounts (employee deductions plus employer CPP and EI) for each remittance period
- **ROE.BLK** files for ROE Web, triggered by interruptions of earnings, with insurable hours and earnings per pay period and any vacation pay paid out
- **T4 slips** and the **T4 electronic filing file** for CRA at year end, combining QuickBooks year-to-date history with Phlo
- WSIB premium figures

### Cut-over

- QuickBooks year-to-date payroll history is imported, including vacation pay balances.
- Payroll runs in parallel (Phlo and QuickBooks) until net pay matches, before QuickBooks payroll is switched off.

## Build Later

1. **Receivables:** client payments matched to invoices, overdue reminders.
2. **Reporting:** loaded margin after employer costs, and profitability by client. Employer costs differ between method A and method B workers.

## Known Issues

| Issue | Impact | Current Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Handwritten timesheets | Slow, error-prone re-keying; OCR unreliable | Manual reading |
| Hours in hours:minutes | Conversion errors | Manual conversion |
| Hours keyed into Excel, then each payment keyed into QuickBooks and again into the bank portal (about 350 a week) | Triple entry, mismatches between pay, bank and billing | Manual checks |
| Bill rate set by adding a margin by hand | Inconsistent margins across invoices | Manual |
| QuickBooks costs about CAD 8,000 a year for payroll and accounts, with per-employee pricing planned | Unsuited to high-turnover staffing | Phase 1: payroll moves to Phlo; QuickBooks kept for accounts |
| ROE sample issued more than a year after employment ended | Late ROEs | None |
| Hours disputes | Rework, worker complaints | Worker initials per day |
