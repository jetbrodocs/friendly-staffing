## Meeting Purpose

Define the scope for Friendly Staffing's new operational system.

## Key Takeaways

- Phased Development: The project will be built in two phases: Phase 1 for core operations (registration, matching, communication) and Phase 2 for payroll and timekeeping.
- Automated Matching: The system will automate candidate matching based on a priority order (e.g., worked at client before) to quickly fill volatile, short-notice orders.
- Timekeeping & Payroll: Phase 2 will introduce a timekeeping module to verify hours, prevent disputes, and generate payroll data, replacing an $8,000/year QuickBooks expense.
- Centralized Database: A single, centralized database will replace the current manual, two-office system, providing a unified view of ~5,000 candidates.

## Topics

### Current Operations & Pain Points

- Manual Processes: All operations (matching, scheduling, communication) are manual, using spreadsheets and WhatsApp.
- Decentralized Data: Two separate office databases create inefficiencies and a fragmented view of the candidate pool.
- Volatile Client Needs: Clients often place urgent, short-notice orders (e.g., 10 people for tomorrow) with frequent schedule changes.
- Communication Bottleneck: Reliance on WhatsApp for job postings is risky due to its potential for future costs or service changes.
- Payroll Cost: QuickBooks' $8,000/year cost is unsustainable for high-volume, temporary staffing, as it charges per employee.

### Proposed System: Phase 1 (Core Operations)

- Employee Portal: For self-registration and viewing job openings.
- Automated Matching & Shortlisting:
  - System filters the candidate database based on job criteria.
  - Shortlists candidates using a priority order to ensure the best fit and reliability.
  - Priority Order Example:
    1. Active employee who has worked at this client before.
    2. Active employee who has worked for Friendly Staffing before.
    3. New candidate.
- Automated Communication:
  - Send bulk SMS/WhatsApp messages to shortlisted candidates.
  - Track responses (e.g., "1" for yes, "0" for no).
  - Alert staff for manual follow-up if no response is received within a set time.
- Client-Specific Instructions:
  - Automatically send confirmed workers client-specific instructions (e.g., GMP rules, safety gear) to prevent on-site issues.
- Client Order Intake:
  - An employer portal for clients to submit job requirements.
  - Challenge: Automating order intake from WhatsApp is difficult due to encryption.
  - Backup Plan: Staff manually copy/paste details from WhatsApp into the system.

### Proposed System: Phase 2 (Timekeeping & Payroll)

- Timekeeping Module:
  - A tablet-based system for client sites to track worker hours.
  - Displays the daily schedule and allows workers to punch in/out.
  - Confirms check-ins with workers via SMS/email to prevent disputes.
  - Automatically calculates total hours, including breaks and overtime.
- Payroll Data Generation:
  - System generates a weekly Excel report with total hours, pay rates, and gross wages.
  - This report will feed into a separate, future payroll software (replacing QuickBooks).
  - Government deductions and taxes will be excluded from Phase 2 scope.
