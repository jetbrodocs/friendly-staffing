- 17 faxed pages.
- Fax header: AUG/17/2026/MON, 08:17–08:26 AM.
- Period: "10th Aug to 15th Aug" (Mon 2026-08-10 to Sat 2026-08-15).

## Activity

Each worker records sign-in and sign-out times per day on a paper sheet at the client site. The worker initials each day. A supervisor signs each page. At week end the sheets are faxed to Friendly Staffing on Monday morning. Friendly Staffing uses them to calculate pay and billing.

## Sheet Layout

- **Header:**
  - Client name ("Molinaros Fine Italian Food").
  - Friendly Staffing's own phone and fax (905-848-1400 / 905-848-8010).
  - The template is Friendly Staffing's, with the client name added.
- **Rows:** 7 worker slots per page, about 115 workers in total across 17 pages. Each slot holds:
  - worker name (handwritten)
  - employee code
  - three rows of times
- **Columns:**
  - Sunday to Saturday, each with "Sign In", "Sign Out" and "Hrs/Sh".
  - Weekly totals at the end in "Reg." and "OT" columns.
- **Totals:** written as hours:minutes, e.g. 44:00 Reg and 04:55 OT. Daily hours are circled, e.g. (9:35).
- **Footer:** "Each Employee must initial to acknowledge In and Out time" and "Supervisor Signature".

## Observations from the Data

- **Days worked:** Most entries fall Monday to Thursday. Friday and Saturday are mostly struck through. This fits Tejash's description of a volatile schedule.
- **Overtime:** Applies above 44 regular hours per week (several workers show 44:00 Reg + OT). This is consistent with Ontario's 44-hour overtime threshold.
- **Hours format:** Hours are recorded in hours:minutes, not decimals.
- **Codes:** Each worker is identified by name plus employee code. Duplicate surnames (Singh, Kaur) are common.

## Problems and Workarounds

| Problem | Frequency | Current Workaround | Impact |
