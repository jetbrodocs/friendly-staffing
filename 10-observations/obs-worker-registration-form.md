## Activity

A new worker fills in a one-page paper form by hand and signs it. Staff verify the worker's documents. When validation is complete and the worker is marked ready to work, staff assign an employee code (per Rohan, 2026-10-26).

## Fields on the Form

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| Identity | First, middle and last name. Date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY). Gender (Female / Male) |
| Address | Street, apartment number, city/province, postal code. **Major intersection** (e.g. "Dundas/Hurontario") |
| Contact | Employee code, email, cell, alternate phone. Emergency contact name, relation and phone |

| Role | Position applied for (free text). Experience/skills (free text) |

| Capability | Driving licence: G2 / G / AZ / DZ. Heavy lifting: 20–40 / 50–80 / 80–100 [unit not printed, likely lb]. Safety shoes Y/N. Own car Y/N |
| Education | High school / college / university |
| Sign-off | Employee signature, date |

## Employee Code Structure

- **Tejash's explanation:**
  - First digit = year (2 = 2022).
  - Next two = month (07 = July).
  - The rest identifies the worker and "who was my associate who registered them".
  - The gap ("207 103") makes the number easier to remember.
- **Evidence from the timesheet and form:**
  - Codes follow this pattern: 608309 on a form dated Aug 2026, 901124, 011007, 512104.
  - The second group often starts with 1, 3 or 7. That suggests the first digit of the last three is the staff member and the last two are a serial. [UNCONFIRMED]
- **Decision (Rohan, Q6):** keep Friendly Staffing's code scheme as is.
